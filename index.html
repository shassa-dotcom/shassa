UI/UX Case Study

Pregnancy Awareness Application

Health & Wellness Mobile UX Awareness App
RoleUI/UX Designer
Tools
PlatformMobile (Android-first)
FocusExpecting Mothers

Project Overview

“LittleNest” is a user-centric pregnancy and nutrition app designed to support expecting mothers throughout their journey. It provides personalized health tracking, expert-backed nutritional guidance, appointment management, and a supportive community, ensuring a seamless and stress-free experience from pregnancy to postpartum.

Problem Statement

A user-friendly platform is needed to provide reliable pregnancy guidance, nutrition support, and symptom awareness for first-time mothers in India.The design challenge was: How do we build a pregnancy companion that is informative yet deeply human?

Key Design Decisions

😌 Warm Visual LanguagePastel gradients and custom illustrations replace harsh medical iconography — creating an emotional connection rather than clinical detachment.
📅 Week-by-Week JourneyBreaking 40 weeks into digestible milestones reduces anxiety and helps users understand exactly where they are in their journey.
🌐 Accessibility-FirstLarge tap targets, high-contrast text, and illustrated content support users with low literacy levels and diverse tech comfort zones.

Screen Highlights

Outcome & Learnings

This project deepened my understanding of designing for emotionally sensitive contexts. The biggest learning was balancing information density with emotional tone — too much data causes anxiety, too little feels patronising. I developed a strong system of visual hierarchy, progressive disclosure, and illustration-based communication that I now apply across projects.

UI/UX Case Study

Rojgaar Application

Employment Mobile App Social Impact
RoleUI/UX Designer
Tools
PlatformMobile (Android-first)
FocusBlue-collar Workers

Project Overview

Rojgaar ("Rojgaar" meaning Job in English) is a mobile job-matching application designed specifically for India's massive informal and blue-collar workforce — daily wage workers, domestic helpers, construction workers, delivery personnel, and small-trade artisans. The app aims to connect them with local employers through a simple, accessible, and language-inclusive interface.

Problem Statement

Over 400 million informal workers in India lack access to formal job platforms. Existing platforms (LinkedIn, Naukri) assume high literacy, English fluency, and white-collar contexts. The challenge: How might we design a job platform that genuinely works for someone with a Class 8 education, a low-end Android phone, and limited data?

Key Design Decisions

🙂Icon-First NavigationReduced text dependency by using universally understood icons and illustrations — enabling non-literate users to navigate effectively.
🫡Hyper-Local FocusJobs filtered by walking/commuting distance first — because informal workers cannot afford long commutes or relocations.
😌Minimal LoadLightweight design optimised for 2G/3G connections and entry-level Android devices with limited storage.

Screen Highlights

Outcome & Learnings

Rojgaar pushed me to design without assumptions. Stripping away everything non-essential taught me that great UX is often about what you remove, not what you add. The project reinforced my belief in designing for the margins — when a product works for the most constrained user, it works beautifully for everyone.
